## Engineering principles

- **Define what must never happen.** Unsafe states should become validation, tests, configuration checks, and operational controls.
- **Protect the source of truth.** AI output is derived context, not authority over customer or lifecycle data.
- **Make failure visible and recoverable.** Record failures, preserve evidence, design retries deliberately, and never report silent success.
- **Own the complete delivery path.** A feature needs testing, deployment, verification, observability, documentation, and rollback.
- **Prefer maintainable handoff over lock-in.** Systems should remain understandable and operable after the original builder steps away.
